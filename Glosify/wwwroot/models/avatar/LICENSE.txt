Rowan's short02 hair and eyebrow009 brows are MakeHuman core assets under CC0.
Adaptation: close-fitting mop with a fringe above the brows, medium-brown strand colour and
head-attached movement morphs. The layered hair locks over it and their strand
texture are original GlobeGlotter geometry generated by build-rain.py. His light brown
stubble, brown brow colour and pale-blue iris tint are original edits to the CC0 skin,
eyebrow and eye textures.

Kwame uses the MakeHuman CC0 middleage_african_male skin, eyebrow003 brows and the
male_elegantsuit01 suit, retextured as a herringbone tweed blazer with an ivory shirt,
oxblood knit tie, elbow patches and gold GG emblem. His close-cropped hair and short beard
shell, its coil texture and his tortoiseshell glasses are original GlobeGlotter geometry
generated by build-kwame.py; the root shading and dark-brown iris tint are original edits
to the CC0 skin and eye textures.

Mei uses the MakeHuman CC0 young_asian_female skin, eyebrow012 brows and the
male_elegantsuit01 suit, slimmed to her figure (shirt and tie removed) and retextured as a
charcoal fine-wool blazer. Her sleek low bun and swept bangs, ivory silk blouse and gold GG
lapel pin are original GlobeGlotter geometry generated by build-mei.py, mei_hair.py and
mei_outfit.py; the root shading, softened skin, baked occlusion, lip tone and dark-brown
iris tint are original edits to the CC0 skin and eye textures.
