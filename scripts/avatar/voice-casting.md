# Avatar voice casting

Reviewed 2026-10-10 against the app’s 69 learning languages, the full 442-voice account catalogue, and language/gender searches in the ElevenLabs voice library.

- **Rain:** teenage or young-adult sound; light, bright and clear, with gentle warmth and natural conversation to match her bookish styling.
- **Rowan:** young-adult, grounded, thoughtful and relaxed; clear medium-to-low delivery to complement the V-neck cardigan.

These are the **shipped voice selections**, chosen from provider metadata. They have not had a listening audition. Language labels and descriptions are provider claims, not independent proof of native pronunciation. Prefer the app’s locale where a comparable candidate exists. Several less-resourced languages have designed voices or no clearly youthful candidate; each is marked in `voice-casting.json` and the audition sheet.

There are language-labelled pairs for **68 languages**, plus a **provisional Georgian multilingual pair** (Isabel / Will) requiring a Georgian listening check. **All 138 assignments (137 unique voices) are now available in the deployment account.** Added 92 missing library voices and pinned both 69-language dictionaries in `Glosify/appsettings.json`. Production uses the same provider credential and has no conflicting voice overrides. The app deliberately rejects unavailable configured IDs before paid work.

The downloadable audition sheet is `output/voice-casting/index.html`. It uses copies of existing provider previews, which can be in a different language from the intended assignment. A matching v4 Turbo language preview is used where the catalogue provides one. `output/voice-casting/proposed-voice-overrides.json` mirrors the shipped mapping, including the explicitly provisional Georgian fallback.

Method: inspect up to 100 popular young voices per language/gender; broaden to adult voices where fewer than three language-labelled matches exist; combine with all available account voices; then review descriptions for character fit and inconsistencies. For Rain’s revision, include teenage-sounding voices, add trending young female searches and broader teen/teenage/youthful/young keyword searches, and inspect descriptions for age and gender contradictions. Exclude old labels, custom-rate and live-moderated voices. This does not establish the age of the real speaker. These searches are a shortlist, not an exhaustive catalogue audit. Georgian was also searched by name; results were unrelated English/Russian voices.

Sources: [account voice catalogue API](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/voices/search), [shared voice catalogue API](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/voices/voice-library/get-shared). Exact voice IDs, provider descriptions, age/accent labels, verification metadata, availability, previews and rationale are in [voice-casting.json](voice-casting.json).
